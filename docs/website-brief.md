# Website brief

## Who we are

Euphemia is a computational drug-discovery company. We make the physics of water
inside protein binding sites computable at campaign scale, so partners can design
molecules with confidence.

Core message: conventional docking treats a binding site as dry, and ignores the
water in it. We model that water explicitly with Grand Canonical Monte Carlo
(GCMC) and molecular dynamics (MD), accelerate it with machine learning, and
validate with rigorous free-energy calculations.

## What the site is for

This is a marketing site for two audiences:

- **Biopharma partners**: teams where water-network modelling is the bottleneck in
  target validation, hit-to-lead, or FEP campaign design.
- **Investors and VC**: a clear, credible explanation of the science and the
  differentiation.

## Sections

- **Hero**: the headline promise, plus an interactive 3D binding-cavity simulation.
- **Science pillars**: Physics (GCMC), Machine Learning, Truth (validation).
- **Approach**: the three-stage discovery pipeline.
- **Target explorer**: interactive case studies of hydration-driven affinity gains.
- **Benchmarks**: quantitative validation claims.
- **Differentiation**: how we compare to docking, pure ML, academic MD, and legacy suites.
- **Contact**: request a target pilot.

## Tone

Factual, precise, restrained. No hype. Every scientific claim must trace to a
reference in `REFERENCES.md` (see the [Science](science.md) page).
